# Ethics and authorization

## Rules for development and testing

1. Use only devices you own, or devices covered by written authorization.
2. Record the authorization reference for every case (the `init` command requires it).
3. Never put real case data in this repository, in screenshots, or in the thesis.
4. Acquire only what the case needs (minimization).
5. Store acquired evidence on encrypted storage with restricted access.
6. Do not publish exploit or lock-bypass code; describe such techniques conceptually only.
7. Obtain ethics committee approval before any study involving real operational data.

## Standards to follow

- ISO/IEC 27037 (identification, collection, acquisition, preservation)
- ISO/IEC 27042 (analysis and interpretation)
- NIST SP 800-101 Rev. 1 (mobile device forensics)
- SWGDE mobile device guidance

## Zambian legal framework (confirm with legal counsel)

Review the Cyber Security Act and Cyber Crimes Act of 2025 and the Data Protection Act of 2021,
with your supervisor and the Zambia Police legal team, for provisions on search, seizure,
evidence handling and personal data. Confirm exact titles and sections before citing them.
